Our Products

Plan3

Plan3 is a passenger disruption management system for airlines, offering automated communication and recovery options like rebooking, hotels, and meal vouchers, allowing passengers to self-serve.

Go to Plan3

Grounded

Grounded is the only airline summit dedicated to passenger disruption management. 150 airline leaders gather in Iceland for two days of interactive panels, workshops, and networking - with the aim of creating better outputs for passenger disruptions.

Go to Grounded

ImPax

The Airline ImPax Report is new monthly research that analyses airline delays and cancellations for the top 290 airlines and the impact on the passenger experience. Included are airline benchmarks and passenger estimates across tier, class and region.

Go to ImPax

Cura

Cura is an AI-powered platform that automates airline passenger compensation claims from submission through resolution, with automated validation, fraud detection, and receipt verification. It integrates with existing airline systems to centralize claims workflows and give passengers self-service options.

Go to Cura
