AI-powered passenger disruption concierge

 

Passengers don’t care about systems, they care about outcomes

During disruption, pax expect one smooth conversation that resolves everything. Most airlines still force them across disconnected channels, teams, and tools.

  • Pax are bounced between rebooking, care, and claims touchpoints
  • Support teams re-check the same details across multiple systems
  • Inventory exists, but isn’t surfaced in one coherent passenger flow
  • Experiences are generic instead of tailored to each traveler context

A concierge layer for every passenger journey

Deploy a passenger concierge that runs on top of Plan3 and Claims, orchestrating options in real time and guiding each pax from disruption to resolution in one branded conversation.

  • Passenger-first: one thread for rebooking, care, and compensation
  • Personalized: adapts tone, options, and priorities per traveler
  • Inventory-rich: combines owned and third-party supply, including paid options

We built a working prototype of this passenger experience: proactive outreach, personalized options, rebooking, duty of care, and claims in one seamless concierge flow.
