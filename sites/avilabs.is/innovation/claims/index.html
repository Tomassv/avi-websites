Claims management and automation

 

Biggest pain points

Today’s claims handling is slow, manual, and frustrating for both agents and passengers. Data lives in silos, and communication feels impersonal.

  • Manual case handling, 15–20 min each
  • Disjointed systems and poor visibility
  • Generic passenger communication
  • Missed loyalty opportunities

Our vision

Smart automation that cuts effort, reduces costs, and improves passenger trust.

  • Fast intake: self-service forms with real-time validation
  • Connected: PSS, OPS, CRM, Finance all linked
  • Fraud-safe: AI checks for duplicates & tampering
  • Passenger-first: empathetic comms, miles or vouchers as goodwill

Claim entry and validation

Manual entry slows everything down and frustrates passengers. Our approach: smarter intake, instant checks, and automation where it matters.

  • Seamless web/app/chatbot submission
  • Real-time validation and duplicate detection
  • Risk-based automation — easy claims closed automatically
  • Personalized forms with a “vent box” to capture emotion

Offering and compensation

Compensation is usually generic and legalistic, missing chances to build loyalty. Our vision: turn every claim into a moment of recovery and trust.

  • Smart offers: miles, vouchers, credits instead of cash only
  • Automated processing: instant offer for standard cases
  • Compliance built-in: EU261, DOT, Montreal handled automatically
  • Clear comms: empathetic tone, transparent timelines

Smarter tools for agents and managers

Claims teams need speed, accuracy, and visibility. Our platform gives agents the automation they need to clear queues quickly, while managers get full oversight and control.

  • Group claims by flight, date, or category
  • Scenario automation — execute multi-step actions in one click
  • Design and test passenger journeys with a no-code, A/B testing builder
  • Batch processing to resolve similar claims faster
  • Custom views by claim type or disruption

Status and communication

Passengers want updates that are fast, clear, and human. Automation should feel empathetic, not robotic — providing timely information, reducing uncertainty, and showing that the airline is in control even when things go wrong.

  • Instant acknowledgment with timelines
  • Strategic follow-ups (not spam)
  • Human override anytime
  • Consistent branding and secure channels
