AI-powered passenger disruption management

 

Biggest pain points

When a flight is cancelled, passengers are left confused and agents are overwhelmed. Generic SMS blasts and call centre queues destroy trust at the worst possible moment.

  • Passengers don’t know their options or rights
  • Agents manually handle hundreds of similar cases
  • Impersonal, one-size-fits-all communication
  • Hotel, meals, and rebooking handled in separate systems

Our vision

A conversational AI that meets each passenger by name, explains their rights, and resolves everything — rebooking, hotel, meals, and claims — in one seamless flow.

  • Personalised: greets passengers by name with their booking context
  • Self-service: passengers choose and confirm without calling anyone
  • End-to-end: flight, hotel, voucher, and claim handled in one chat
  • Compliant: EU261 entitlements surfaced automatically

We’ve built a fully working prototype of this passenger disruption flow — from the cancellation notice all the way through rebooking, hotel, meal voucher, and EU261 claim.

Instant disruption notification

The moment a flight is cancelled, the passenger receives a personalised message — addressed by name, with their booking reference and cancelled route already loaded. No searching, no hold music.

  • Passenger greeted by name with full booking context pre-loaded
  • Cancellation status and reason communicated empathetically
  • Three clear options presented immediately: rebook, pick a new date, or refund
  • Chat open for any question at any point in the flow

Rebooking options that actually work

Instead of forcing passengers to call in, the AI presents three tailored options they can action themselves — and handles confirmation in real time.

  • Next available flight: shows the next departure and lets the passenger accept in one tap
  • Flexible rebook: any same-route flight within 90 days, no change fee
  • Full refund or travel credit: 100% refund or credit with a 30% loyalty bonus
  • Alternative airlines surfaced with fare-difference payment handled in chat

Meals, hotels, and care — all in one flow

A cancelled flight often means hours or a night stuck at the airport. The AI proactively handles passenger welfare without any agent involvement.

  • Meal voucher sent automatically with a curated list of eligible airport restaurants
  • Hotel offers surfaced when the next flight is more than a day away
  • Three hotel options with photos, room type, board basis, and check-in details
  • Step-by-step itinerary delivered to the passenger — shuttle stop, boarding gate, bag drop deadline

EU261 and compensation — handled inside the chat

Passengers often don't know their rights. The AI surfaces EU261 eligibility automatically, explains what applies to their specific disruption, and guides them through a structured claim if they choose to proceed.

  • Eligibility determined automatically based on the disruption reason
  • Claim type and amount selected through guided conversation
  • Standard 200 EUR claim processed instantly; higher amounts escalated with upload instructions
  • All confirmations sent to the passenger's email with a case reference
