New EU Passenger Rights

Notify every passenger within 96 hours — automatically

The EU's landmark reform of air passenger rights changes what airlines must do when a flight is disrupted. Cura's Disruption Rights Outreach turns those new obligations into a workflow that runs itself — so you stay compliant without adding headcount.

What's changing
Proactive 96-hour outreach
Airlines will now have to reach out first — notifying every affected passenger within 96 hours. Cura scans each day's flights and sends the outreach automatically, well inside the window, with no one added to the ops team.
Honest, in context
The real reason, up front
Cura reads why each flight was disrupted and tells passengers exactly where they stand — including an immediate heads-up when extraordinary circumstances, like weather or ATC, mean no compensation is due. Fewer dead-end claims, fewer disputes.
How Cura helps
The right message, every time
Every email is matched to the passenger's exact entitlement. And for those caught in the disruption but not owed compensation, Cura can add a goodwill gesture — like 10% off their next flight — turning a bad day into retained loyalty.
