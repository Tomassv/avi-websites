Nine capabilities that shape how passengers file, experience, and resolve their claim.

9 value propositions
01

Claim Intake — PSS Integration

Eliminates invalid claims Reduces fraud No manual entry

Passenger and booking data are automatically retrieved from the airline's reservation system, eliminating manual entry errors. Invalid, duplicate, and fraudulent claims are stopped before they enter the compensation workflow — reducing abuse by claim agencies and creating a faster filing experience for genuine passengers.

02

Verify Every Passenger

Email confirmation e-signature Audit trail

Each passenger in a claim must confirm their participation before it proceeds. Reduces unauthorized claims and agency abuse — and gives airlines an early window to offer credits before passengers turn to third parties.

03

Intelligent Claiming

Dynamic claim types Fewer duplicates Instant feedback

Claim types are dynamically enabled or restricted based on known flight and passenger data. Passengers receive instant feedback while filing — deterring ineligible submissions and reducing total claim volume before cases even open.

04

Let Passengers Be Heard

Free-form input Sentiment capture Zero agent workload

Every claim includes an optional space for passengers to share their story. Airlines gain direct insight into pain points and service failures — without adding workload to claims teams.

05

Guide Toward Realistic Compensation

Regulation-based guidance Fewer disputes

When requested amounts differ significantly from expected compensation, passengers receive clear guidance about applicable regulations and available alternatives — resulting in better-informed submissions and faster resolution.

06

Keep Passengers Informed

Self-service tracking Automated updates Fewer inbound contacts

Automatically communicates next steps, expected processing times, and claim status updates throughout the resolution journey. Giving passengers transparency and self-service access significantly reduces avoidable customer support interactions while improving the overall experience.

07

Smart Compensation Offers

Credits & miles Higher perceived value Lower cash cost

Airlines can offer travel credits, vouchers, or loyalty miles instead of cash at any stage. A win-win: passengers receive greater perceived value while airlines reduce compensation costs and increase future bookings.

08

Retaining Loyalty with Goodwill

Ineligible claims Discount codes Retention tool

When a claim isn't eligible for compensation, airlines can still offer discount codes, travel vouchers, or bonus miles — turning a potentially negative experience into a retention and future booking opportunity.

09

Automated Payment Collection

IBAN & bank details Fewer follow-ups Faster payouts

Required payment details are collected and validated automatically at the right stage of the journey — eliminating back-and-forth and reducing processing delays on the final payout.

02
Automation & Intelligence

Back-office power
& operational insight

Six capabilities that automate operations, ensure compliance, and surface actionable data.

6 value propositions
10

End-to-End Claims Automation

Any step or full workflow Deterministic rules AI as assistant only

Airlines can automate end-to-end handling or only selected steps while keeping manual oversight where needed. Unlike systems that rely on AI to make decisions, Cura uses deterministic workflows based on airline policies, regulations, and business rules — so every claim is handled consistently, transparently, and compliantly.

11

Purpose-Built for Airline Claims

Single source of truth Flight-level eligibility Full audit trail

Passenger, booking, flight, disruption, communication, and document data in one platform. Eligibility can be assessed once at the flight level and automatically applied across all related claims — eliminating system switching and reducing investigation time.

12

Automated Regulatory & Legal Responses

NEB & court responses Evidence packages AI-assisted drafting

When transportation authorities or legal representatives request information, Cura can automatically gather evidence, consolidate case history, and prepare a complete response package — with optional agent review before submission.

13

AI-Powered Claim Assistance

Document analysis Fraud detection Airlines stay in control

AI analyzes receipts, invoices, and baggage images, detects fraud, drafts communications, and recommends actions — while keeping airlines fully in control of decisions, compliance, and customer outcomes.

14

Monitor & Act on Customer Sentiment

Social & review tracking AI categorization Convert to claims

Monitors social media and review platforms to identify passenger concerns and reputation risks. AI categorizes mentions, analyzes sentiment, and enables teams to convert public complaints into actionable claims — directly from the platform.

15

Turn Claims Data into Operational Insight

Compensation cost visibility Disruption trends AI-powered analytics

Airline-specific reporting across claims, operations, customer experience, and compensation costs. Teams can track claim volumes, processing performance, and disruption trends — while AI-powered analytics uncover patterns, detect anomalies, and recommend areas for operational improvement. Data that is impossible to get from disconnected systems.
