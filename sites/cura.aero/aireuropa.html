Honest constraints

What we need from you

  • Booking and boarding records — the first two items AESA asks for. Without them there is no filing.
  • Delay codes and the operations log — the cause, and the evidence of reasonable measures
  • Maintenance records — only where a technical cause is claimed
  • Eurocontrol operator credentials — slot and flow messages, which only you can authorise
  • A named approver — one person who signs off a filing

This is the longest lead time in the project and it is unaffected by anything else. We would rather raise it in the first meeting than the third.
