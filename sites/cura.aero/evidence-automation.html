How it works

Data in. Filed package out.

When a passenger escalates to the authority or to court, this is every source that goes into the file, who supplies it, and what happens next. Every source is fetched over an API — the only thing anyone at the airline does is approve the response for sending.

Stage one

Intake and collection

A request arrives with a reply deadline. Cura reads it and matches it to the claim — or opens one, if the passenger never claimed with the airline and went straight to the authority. Then every evidence source is pulled at once over an API. Nothing here is a request to a person, and nothing waits on one; the connections below are set up once, at onboarding.

In practice

What they ask for, and what goes back

A real request lists its items and expects each one answered. The package mirrors that list item for item, so the case handler on the other side can tick their way down it.

Request for documentation — case ref. EU-2026-04178
From
passengerrights@authority.example
To
claims@airline.example
Date
12 March 2026 · reply due 11 April

Dear Sir or Madam,

We have received a complaint concerning the diversion and subsequent delay of the above flight. In order to properly progress with this matter, we kindly request that you provide the following documentation:

  1. All relevant slot messages, including the original CTOT, any revised CTOTs, and related NOTAMs
  2. All NOTAMs valid at the relevant time for the route, departure airport, destination airport, and alternate/diversion airport
  3. All relevant Eurocontrol messages, communications, or network management notifications that influenced the CTOT allocation, delay, or diversion decision
  4. Any other operational messages, weather reports, or internal communications that may be material to understanding the cause and handling of the diversion and delay

Please note that a failure to reply within the period stated above may lead us to determine the matter on the basis of the information already before us.

Yours faithfully,
Case Officer, Passenger Rights Unit
