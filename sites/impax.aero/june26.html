Do You Know Your Airline's ImPax Percentage?

Passengers that experience cancellations, or medium and major delays without a quick resolution, produce almost 100% of an airline’s negative media that leads to customer churn.

This small percentage of passengers is an airline’s ImPax Percentage.

An airline’s ImPax Percentage is calculated as:


On average, an airline’s ImPax Percentage accounts for less than 1 in 13 flights (< 8%).

The Lower an Airline’s ImPax %, the Better.

The aim of this report is to help airlines focus their recovery operations by better understanding their ImPax Percentage in the context of:

  • Passenger estimates
  • Competitor airlines
  • Region, Tier, and Class
  • Sentiment analysis of monthly reviews

Global Disruptions Overview

This month’s report analyses the disruption data of 246 airlines totalling 715,872 disrupted flights, across 2,742,051 planned flights, globally.

June marked a challenging start to the peak summer travel season as geopolitical tensions, adverse weather, ATC disruption and constrained airspace combined to drive operational pressure across every major region. While flight volumes remained resilient, disruption intensified as airlines entered their busiest period with limited operational slack. Tier 1 carriers bore the brunt of this pressure, accounting for 60% of global flight volumes but nearly 80% of all disrupted flights. As a result, almost 24 million passengers experienced major delays or cancellations during the month, highlighting how disruption continues to concentrate around the world's largest and most interconnected airline networks.

Flight volumes increased by almost 5% in June as Europe entered the peak summer travel season, but operational performance deteriorated sharply.
