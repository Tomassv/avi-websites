Welcome to the Airline ImPax Report

Each month we analyse hundreds of thousands of airline irregular operations and their impact on the passenger experience. The aim of the Airline ImPax Report is to help airlines identify which disruptions impact the passenger experience most.

Included in the report:

  • Ranking of the top 250 airlines using the ImPax Percentage.
  • Total percentage of airline disruptions by tier, class, region, and size of disruption.
  • Estimated impacted passengers per airlines
  • Passenger sentiment analysis per region
